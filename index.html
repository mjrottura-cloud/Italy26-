▲
Read this one carefully — it's the tightest day of the trip. Aug 23 asks you to check out of Rome, cross to Pompeii, tour it, then get to Capri, all in one day.

The timing: Rome checkout is 10:00 AM, but the Pompeii tour starts at 10:30 AM — you cannot do both. Rome to Pompeii is roughly 2 to 2½ hours door to door (high-speed train to Naples, then the local line out to Pompeii). To make a 10:30 start you'd need to leave the penthouse around 6:45–7:00 AM and check out early. That's allowed — you just can't linger to the 10:00 checkout.

The luggage: you'll be moving with everyone's bags. Pompeii doesn't let large luggage into the site; there's a deposit at the entrance, but it has size limits and five people's suitcases is a lot. Confirm with the tour operator how they want you to handle bags before the day arrives — this is the detail most likely to go wrong.

After the tour: you still need to reach Capri. Plan the ferry from Naples or Sorrento with real margin — an afternoon crossing, not the last one.

Before you go

Need to know

Passports
All five valid at least 3 months beyond Aug 28, and names matching tickets exactly. One traveler is named Rome and so is a city — always read carefully so a person and a place don't get crossed.
Border: EES & ETIAS
The EU's biometric entry system (EES) is now live — expect fingerprints & a photo on arrival, so allow extra time at passport control at FCO. ETIAS isn't required yet for these dates, but verify close to departure.
Trains & ferries
Book the high-speed legs and the Amalfi ferries early — late August fills up, and Italian high-speed tickets are tied to a specific departure, so a missed train can mean a lost ticket.
Timed tickets
Museum and monument entries (Uffizi, Accademia, Colosseum) are timed slots — miss the window and you often lose the ticket. Treat them as fixed anchors.
